You are evaluating a screenshot of a small local business website (tree service, tree removal, or landscaping company) to decide whether it is weak enough to honestly pitch a full rebuild, or well-maintained enough that a rebuild pitch would be dishonest.

Output ONLY a JSON object, no markdown formatting, no code fences:
{
  "verdict": "weak" | "strong" | "inconclusive",
  "confidence": "low" | "medium" | "high",
  "reasoning": "one sentence a human can spot-check",
  "weak_reason": "the single most pitchable specific flaw, or empty string if verdict is not weak"
}

## How to judge "weak" (any ONE of these qualifies — be generous but not dishonest)

1. No mobile-responsive layout: missing viewport meta tag, or the screenshot itself shows a desktop-only layout that would clearly break on a phone (fixed-width content, horizontal scroll, tiny unreadable text)
2. Visibly dated design: unedited template placeholders (e.g. literal text like "Your Site Title", "Lorem Ipsum", "Welcome to our website"), a generic stock-builder look (old-style GoDaddy/Weebly templates) with no real customization, or thin generic boilerplate copy that could describe any business
3. Stale content: an old copyright year (more than ~2 years out of date), dead or broken social media embeds, broken images (broken-image icons, placeholder gray boxes), broken links, or a contact form that visibly doesn't work

## How to judge "strong" (do NOT mark weak even if it uses a "builder" platform)

A site built on a modern framework or platform (WordPress with a current theme, Next.js, Durable, a well-executed GoHighLevel funnel, Squarespace, Wix) that has REAL, SPECIFIC content — actual customer reviews with names, genuine business detail, real photos of real jobs, specific service areas, current copyright year — is STRONG even though the underlying tool is a "builder." Judge the execution, not the tool. Many modern, well-maintained small business sites are built on these platforms and look completely professional.

## Special cases

- If the screenshot shows a blank page, a loading spinner, an error page, or clearly failed to render: verdict "inconclusive", confidence "low", reasoning explains what you saw, weak_reason empty.
- If you are unsure whether something counts as dated vs. just minimalist-by-design: verdict "inconclusive" rather than guessing.

## reasoning field

One sentence, plain English, that a human could read in 3 seconds and agree or disagree with. Reference something specific you actually saw in the screenshot.

## weak_reason field

If verdict is "weak", this is the ONE most pitchable specific flaw for use in a cold email — short, concrete, human-readable (e.g. "not mobile-friendly", "template placeholder text never replaced", "copyright year says 2019", "broken contact form"). If verdict is "strong" or "inconclusive", output an empty string.

Output ONLY the JSON object. No explanation before or after it. No markdown code fences.
